# jude chapter 1

**1:1** yo it's jude fr fr, servant of jesus christ and brother of james, writing to y'all who are absolutely sanctified by the cosmic ceo the father and preserved in jesus christ and called no cap - the tea is you're chosen ong.

**1:2** mercy to you bestie and peace and love be absolutely multiplied fr fr - it's giving divine abundance energy no cap, y'all need this ong.

**1:3** beloved when i was giving all my energy to write to you about our common salvation fr fr, the tea is it became absolutely necessary for me to write and tell you no cap: y'all need to earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered to the saints ong - it's giving fight for truth energy.

**1:4** because certain people have crept in unnoticed fr fr who were long ago marked out for this condemnation bestie - absolutely ungodly individuals no cap turning the grace of our cosmic ceo into a license for debauchery ong and denying our only master and boss jesus christ - the tea is y'all are cooked if you follow them.

**1:5** fr fr i want to remind you (though you once knew this fully bestie) that the boss, having saved the people out of egypt no cap, afterward absolutely destroyed those who didn't believe ong - the tea is unbelief gets you wrecked.

**1:6** and the angels who didn't keep their original position fr fr but abandoned their proper dwelling bestie - he has absolutely kept them in eternal chains under darkness no cap for the judgment of the great day ong, it's giving permanent consequence energy and they're cooked.

**1:7** just like sodom and gomorrah fr fr and the surrounding cities bestie which in the same way gave themselves to sexual immorality and pursued absolutely unnatural lust no cap - they're set forth as an example ong suffering the vengeance of eternal fire, the tea is y'all are seeing the preview of judgment.

**1:8** yet in the same way fr fr these dreamers also defile the flesh bestie, reject authority absolutely, and blaspheme glorious beings no cap - it's giving delusional heretic energy ong and they're absolutely cooked.

**1:9** but even michael the archangel fr fr when he was disputing with the devil about moses' body bestie didn't dare bring a slanderous judgment no cap but said "the boss rebuke you" ong - the tea is even archangels show respect to authority and these fools don't.

**1:10** fr fr but these people blaspheme anything they don't understand bestie and what they do understand absolutely naturally like irrational animals no cap - by these things they're destroyed ong, it's giving willful ignorance gets you cooked energy.

**1:11** woe to them fr fr because they've gone the way of cain bestie and rushed absolutely headlong into balaam's error for profit no cap and perished in korah's rebellion ong - the tea is they're hitting every biblical L possible and y'all are absolutely cooked if you're like them.

**1:12** these are the ones who are absolutely unhinged blemishes at your love feasts fr fr feasting with you without fear bestie shameless shepherds feeding only themselves no cap - they're waterless clouds carried by winds ong, fruitless trees in late autumn twice dead and uprooted, it's giving maximum useless energy.

**1:13** fr fr they're wild waves of the sea bestie foaming up their own absolutely shameful deeds no cap, wandering stars ong for whom the blackness of darkness has been reserved forever - the tea is eternal L incoming and y'all are cooked.

**1:14** enoch the seventh from adam fr fr prophesied about these people bestie saying "look the boss is coming absolutely with ten thousands of his holy ones no cap - it's giving apocalyptic judgment energy ong."

**1:15** fr fr to execute judgment on all bestie and to convict all the absolutely ungodly no cap of all their ungodly deeds they've committed in an ungodly way ong and of all the harsh things ungodly sinners have spoken against him - the tea is the receipts are coming and y'all are cooked.

**1:16** these people are absolutely unhinged grumblers and complainers fr fr walking according to their own evil desires bestie - their mouth speaks pompous words no cap showing favoritism to gain advantage ong, it's giving toxic influencer energy.

**1:17** but you beloved fr fr remember the words previously spoken by the apostles of our boss jesus christ bestie - they absolutely told you this was coming no cap ong.

**1:18** the tea is they said to you fr fr "in the last time there will be absolutely unhinged mockers bestie walking according to their own ungodly desires no cap" - y'all were warned ong.

**1:19** these are the people who cause divisions fr fr worldly-minded bestie not having the spirit absolutely no cap - it's giving spiritually dead energy ong and they're cooked.

**1:20** but you beloved fr fr building yourselves up on your most holy faith bestie praying in the holy ghost absolutely no cap - it's giving spiritual construction energy ong, this is how y'all stay based.

**1:21** fr fr keep yourselves in the love of the cosmic ceo bestie waiting for the mercy of our boss jesus christ absolutely leading to eternal life no cap - the tea is this is the survival strategy ong.

**1:22** and have mercy on some fr fr who are doubting bestie making a distinction absolutely no cap - it's giving discernment energy ong, not everyone gets the same approach.

**1:23** fr fr save others by snatching them out of the fire bestie and to others show mercy absolutely with fear no cap hating even the garment stained by the flesh ong - the tea is rescue the perishing but protect yourselves, it's giving emergency spiritual extraction energy.

**1:24** now to him who is able fr fr to keep you from stumbling bestie and to present you absolutely faultless before the presence of his glory no cap with exceeding joy ong - it's giving ultimate security energy, he's got y'all.

**1:25** to the only wise cosmic ceo our savior fr fr be glory and majesty bestie dominion and power absolutely no cap both now and forever ong - amen and amen, the tea is he's the eternal winner and if you're with him y'all are not cooked.

# genesis chapter 1

**1:1** in the beginning god created the heaven and the earth - literally speaking everything into existence from nothing, ex nihilo vibes fr fr.

**1:2** the earth was without form and void, darkness on the face of the deep. but the spirit of god moved upon the face of the waters.

**1:3** and god said "let there be light" and there was light. spoken word just hits different when you're the cosmic ceo.

**1:4** god saw the light was good and divided the light from the darkness.

**1:5** he called the light day and the darkness night. evening and morning, first day.

**1:6** and god said "let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, dividing the waters from the waters."

**1:7** so god made the firmament and divided the waters under from the waters above, and it was so.

**1:8** god called the firmament heaven. evening and morning, second day.

**1:9** god said "let the waters under heaven gather to one place and let dry land appear" and it was so.

**1:10** he called the dry land earth and the gathered waters seas. god saw that it was good.

**1:11** then god said "let the earth bring forth grass, herb yielding seed, fruit tree yielding fruit after his kind" and it was so.

**1:12** earth brought forth grass and herbs and trees, all yielding seed after their kind. god saw it was good.

**1:13** evening and morning, third day.

**1:14** god said "let there be lights in the firmament of heaven to divide day from night. let them be for signs and seasons and days and years."

**1:15** "let them give light upon the earth" and it was so.

**1:16** god made two great lights - greater light to rule the day, lesser light to rule the night. he made the stars also. just casually creating the entire solar system.

**1:17** he set them in the firmament to give light on earth.

**1:18** to rule over day and night, to divide light from darkness. god saw it was good.

**1:19** evening and morning, fourth day.

**1:20** god said "let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that has life, and fowl that fly above the earth."

**1:21** god created great whales and every living creature that moves in the waters and every winged fowl after their kind. he saw that it was good.

**1:22** god blessed them saying "be fruitful and multiply. fill the waters in the seas. let fowl multiply in the earth."

**1:23** evening and morning, fifth day.

**1:24** god said "let the earth bring forth living creature after his kind - cattle and creeping thing and beast of the earth" and it was so.

**1:25** god made the beasts and cattle and everything that creeps on earth after their kind. he saw it was good.

**1:26** then god said "let us make man in our image, after our likeness" - okay wait the plural pronouns here? "let *us* make man in *our* image"? this is the imago dei moment, humans getting made in the image of the divine, and there's already hints of trinity energy happening in genesis 1. let them have dominion over the fish and the fowl and the cattle and all the earth.

**1:27** so god created man in his own image. in the image of god created he him. male and female created he them. this is literally the foundation for all human dignity - everyone bears the divine image no cap.

**1:28** and god blessed them and said "be fruitful and multiply. replenish the earth and subdue it. have dominion over every living thing." the creation mandate - humans get stewardship of the planet.

**1:29** god said "i've given you every herb bearing seed and every tree with fruit yielding seed. it's yours for food."

**1:30** "and to every beast and fowl and everything that creeps on earth, i've given every green herb for food" and it was so.

**1:31** god saw everything he had made. and behold, it was very good. evening and morning, the sixth day - creation week complete and the boss said it's all immaculate.

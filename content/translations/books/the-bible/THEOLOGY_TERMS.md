## usage guidelines

### consistency rules
1. **first use**: introduce brainrot equivalent alongside traditional term
   - example: "covenant (divine unbreakable contract) between god and abraham"
2. **subsequent uses**: can use brainrot term alone once established
   - example: "this divine contract applies to all descendants"
3. **theological precision**: ensure meaning remains clear despite modern wrapping
4. **density contribution**: these terms count toward 3-5 brainrot terms per sentence requirement

### context-appropriate selection
- **reverent moments**: lean toward respectful descriptors ("ultimate authority figure" not "cosmic ceo" for serious prayer)
- **teaching moments**: use clearer equivalents that illuminate meaning
- **dialogue**: match character voice from BIBLICAL_VOICES.md
- **dramatic moments**: allow emotional descriptors that heighten impact

### combining with character voices
theological terms work with character signature terms:
- **god speaking**: "this covenant is fr fr ong unbreakable"
- **paul writing**: "literally saved by grace, absolutely not by works"
- **prophets warning**: "repentance = doing a full 180 before judgment hits"

### avoiding theological error
while maximizing brainrot density, never:
- **change core doctrine**: grace must remain unmerited, faith must involve trust
- **add beliefs not in text**: no modern theology backdoors
- **trivialize sacrifice**: crucifixion terms retain gravity despite modern wrapping
- **confuse persons of trinity**: maintain distinct roles
